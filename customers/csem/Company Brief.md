## Company background
CSEM is a Swiss public-private non-profit R&D center founded in 1984, with ~650 employees spanning digital technologies, precision manufacturing, and sustainable energy. Its core mission is to develop and transfer technology to industry, which makes it both an end-user and a potential multiplier of any tooling it adopts. CSEM runs an active Edge AI & Vision practice — its publicly profiled work includes TinyML research and the ultra-low-power "MOODY" coin-cell vision system, signaling deep in-house competence in exactly Edge Impulse's domain ([CSEM TinyML](https://www.csem.ch/en/news/tiny-ml-for-decentralized-and-energy-efficient-ai/)).

## Retention assessment
Renewal is comfortably out (2027-09-28), so near-term contractual churn risk is low. The real risk is silent onboarding failure: a Neutral health score of 45 on a brand-new account, no CSM assigned, and an unresolved internal question about whether CS even participates. As a sophisticated R&D house with its own Edge AI team, CSEM can readily decide the platform adds little and quietly disengage well before renewal — and a stalled kickoff is the classic leading indicator. The handoff confusion (Harry/Spencer) needs to be closed immediately so the account does not drift through its onboarding window unowned.
