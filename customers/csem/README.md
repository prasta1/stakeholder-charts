## Overview
CSEM is a Swiss research and development center focused on digital technologies, precision manufacturing, and sustainable energy systems. Operating as a public-private non-profit partnership with over 650 employees across Switzerland, CSEM develops and transfers world-class solutions to reinforce industrial competitiveness.
