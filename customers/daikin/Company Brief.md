## Company background
Daikin Manufacturing Germany is the German manufacturing arm of the Daikin Group, the global HVAC and refrigeration leader, producing heating/cooling systems for European commercial and residential markets. The parent company is leaning hard into AI and IoT: in April 2025 Daikin and Hitachi began trial operation of an AI agent for factory equipment failure diagnostics, and Daikin is pushing an "Air-as-a-Service" model built on predictive analytics and equipment-health monitoring ([Daikin press](https://www.daikin.com/press/2025/20250422)). This strategic direction aligns directly with on-device ML for HVAC condition monitoring.
