## Company background
Getac, founded in 1989, builds rugged tablets and laptops for defense, public safety, utilities, and logistics. The company is actively repositioning around edge AI: it launched a portfolio of Microsoft Copilot+ rugged PCs (F120, B360 Plus, UX10, S510AD) for offline, on-device AI; was named to CRN's 2025 Edge Computing 100; and in April 2026 launched the AMD-powered G140 rugged Copilot+ tablet (available June 2026). Reported revenue was up ~15.6% YoY in Nov 2025, with strong projected 2026 rugged-PC demand ([Getac CRN news](https://www.getac.com/us/news/getac-named-to-the-prestigious-2025-crn-edge-computing/); [Yahoo Finance — G140](https://finance.yahoo.com/sectors/technology/articles/getac-redefines-rugged-mobility-launch-010000512.html)). Their public messaging — "AI where connectivity is limited or nonexistent" — is squarely Edge Impulse territory.

## Edge Impulse relationship
Early-stage and under-instrumented in the CRM: Stage and Maturity are both blank, there is no assigned solutions engineer, and **no active CSM**. Health is Neutral (60), the strongest of the at-risk accounts, but that number is hard to trust given how little is recorded.

## Retention assessment
Renewal is **~4 months out (2026-10-09)** — the nearest renewal in this batch — and the account is essentially unmanaged: no CSM, no SE, no recorded stage/maturity, and still mislabeled as a prospect. A neutral-but-thin health score plus an imminent renewal plus zero CS coverage is a quiet-churn risk. The mismatch between Getac's strong public edge-AI momentum and EI's near-zero engagement is itself the warning sign: the strategic fit is excellent, but nobody on the EI side is positioned to convert it or to defend the renewal.

## Expansion opportunities
- **Embed EI into Getac's rugged edge-AI product line** — their Copilot+ devices (F120, G140, UX10) are platforms that run on-device models in the field; EI could power vertical detection/classification use cases for their defense, public-safety, and utility customers.
- **OEM / partner motion**: as a device maker, Getac is a potential channel that brings EI to its end customers, not just an end user — a far larger TAM than a single seat count.
- **Multi-vertical use cases** (defense, public safety, logistics) each justify additional projects and seats.
- **PS hours** to stand up a first reference use case quickly and prove the device + EI story.

## Recommended next actions
1. **Reclassify the account** from "Prospect" to active customer and fix the CRM record (stage, maturity, SE).
2. **Assign a CSM immediately** — a 4-month renewal with no owner is unacceptable.
3. Run discovery to capture a real use case and validate whether the $25K is being used; tie it to a Copilot+ device line.
4. Explore the OEM/channel angle with Getac product leadership — this is the high-upside path.
5. Open the renewal conversation now given the October date.
