## Company background
Globalsense (founded 2021, Solana Beach, CA) builds AI-powered acoustic sensing for vehicle, asset, and home safety — ultra-affordable, energy-efficient AI at the endpoint, including baby-cry and dog-bark detection and acoustic sensors for fleet/vehicle crash and theft detection. The company is co-founded by Mehdi Chitgari and Saeid Safavi, runs a subscription B2B model, and has signaled expansion ambitions into industrial IoT, HVAC, and oil & gas. Public sources indicate no institutional funding raised yet, though it has a manufacturing partner for mass production — a signal to watch on runway ([Tracxn](https://tracxn.com/d/companies/globalsense/__RpeWeN4XOGVPoe5qUn9kf5elTh1f6qE36hNt9_26zRU)). Globalsense is already a public Edge Impulse case study for automotive diagnostics ([EI case study](https://www.edgeimpulse.com/case-studies/globalsense-drives-new-standards-in-automotive-diagnostics-with-edge-ai)).

## Retention assessment
Low risk and well-managed; renewal is comfortably distant (2027-07-31). The two things to actively monitor: (1) the post-conference momentum lull noted on 10/14 — advocacy accounts can cool quietly if engagement isn't sustained; and (2) the funding/runway question — an early-stage, not-yet-funded startup carries inherent financial risk to a ~$45K CARR. Neither is acute, but both warrant light, ongoing attention so the relationship doesn't drift and so EI is positioned ahead of any budget pressure.
