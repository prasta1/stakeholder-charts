# Globalsense, Inc.

**Industry:** AI / IoT / Acoustic Sensing
**Headquarters:** Solana Beach, California
**Founded:** 2021

## Overview
Globalsense develops AI-powered acoustic sensing systems for vehicle, asset, and home safety applications. The company specializes in ultra-affordable and energy-efficient AI integration into endpoints and sensors for IoT innovation, with products including baby crying and dog barking detection systems, and advanced acoustic sensors for fleet management.
