## Company background
Halma is a FTSE 100 diversified safety-technology holding company founded in 1894, with ~8,000 employees across 20+ countries and brands in safety, fire detection/suppression, security, medical devices, and environmental monitoring. Its growth model is acquisition-led plus organic expansion within tight technology clusters: it reported record FY2025/26 results (profit up 16.0% to £124.8m, 16.3% organic growth) and acquired Safetec, an Italian industrial fire & gas safety provider, in January 2026 ([Halma FY25/26 results](https://www.halma.com/news/press-releases/2026/full-year-results-2025-26); [Safetec acquisition](https://www.halma.com/news/press-releases/2026/halma-expands-its-industrial-safety-capabilities)). The decentralized operating-company structure is the key to both the risk and the opportunity here.
