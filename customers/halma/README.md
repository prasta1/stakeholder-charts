# Halma plc

**Industry:** Safety & Life Protection Products
**Headquarters:** Amersham, Buckinghamshire, UK
**Founded:** 1894
**Website:** https://www.halma.com

## Overview
Halma is a global diversified holding company operating through multiple brands specializing in life protection products. The company manufactures and distributes safety systems, fire detection and suppression solutions, security products, medical devices, healthcare assessment tools, and environmental monitoring equipment. Halma operates in over 20 countries with approximately 8,000 employees and is listed on the London Stock Exchange as an FTSE 100 constituent.
