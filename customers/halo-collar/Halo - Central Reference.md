## Technical Problem

Halo is struggling with **dog activity classification**. Suspected root causes (likely multifaceted):

1. **Poor dataset collection practices** — data quality issues at capture
2. **Imprecise data handling** — processing/labeling problems
3. **Lab-to-field performance gap** — models that work in testing fail in real-world deployment
4. **Hardware constraints** — collar positioning may affect accelerometer readings

Teague prepared a diagnostic spreadsheet to structure the troubleshooting. Team approach is iterative — no black-box solutions.
