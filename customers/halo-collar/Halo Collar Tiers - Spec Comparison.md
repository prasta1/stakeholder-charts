# Halo Collar — Product Tier Spec Comparison

*Compiled for Edge Impulse CS / account work · Generated 2026-08-05*

Two "tier" axes exist for Halo and both appear in customer conversations:

1. **Hardware tiers** — the collar generations (Collar 4 vs Collar 5)
2. **Pack Membership tiers** — the subscription plans (Bronze / Silver / Gold)

> **Data confidence note:** Hardware specs below are sourced from Halo's own support center ("What's New with the Halo Collar 5", "Halo Collar 4 & 5 Overview", "Dimensions and Weight") plus a 2026 third-party comparison (SmartPetGears). The internal `competitive_analysis_halo_collar.md` in this folder is **stale on pricing** — it lists Gen 4 at $549 MSRP / $424 sale and says Gen 5 pricing "not disclosed." Current 2026 listings put Collar 4 at ~$449 and Collar 5 at ~$599. Membership tier details are confirmed verbatim from Halo's support article (Bronze/Silver/Gold plan options).

## 1. Hardware Tiers — Collar 4 vs Collar 5

| Spec | Halo Collar 4 | Halo Collar 5 |
|---|---|---|
| **Position in lineup** | Value / mid tier ("Budget King" in 2026) | Flagship |
| **2026 price (typical)** | ~$449 | ~$599 |
| **GPS technology** | Dual-frequency L1/L5 | AlwaysOn™ dual-band (L1/L5) + AI filtering |
| **Update rate** | 1 update / second (1 Hz) | 20 updates / second (20 Hz) |
| **Precision+ (Skylark L1+L5 corrections)** | Not available | Available (subscription-gated accuracy boost) |
| **Battery life (GPS active)** | ~30 hours | Up to 48 hours |
| **Charge time** | 2–3 hours (magnetic snap-on dock) | ~1 hour (rapid charge, redesigned dock) |
| **AlwaysOn (no sleep mode)** | No | Yes |
| **On-board AI (behavior detection)** | Yes | Yes (same training system) |
| **Wi-Fi / Bluetooth** | Combined chip | Separated dedicated Wi-Fi + Bluetooth chips |
| **Water resistance** | IPX7 (per internal competitive analysis) | IP67-rated (dust-tight + immersion) |
| **Dimensions (case)** | 6.8" L × 1.25" W × 1.0" T (17.27 × 3.18 × 2.54 cm) | Not separately published; shares Collar 4 design family |
| **Weight (case)** | 0.3 oz (8.51 g) | Not separately published; shares Collar 4 design family |
| **Total weight w/ strap** | 5.9 oz (167 g) | Not separately published; shares Collar 4 design family |
| **Charging style** | Magnetic snap-on dock | Magnetic snap-on dock |
| **LTE cellular** | Yes (unlimited cellular data on any plan) | Yes (unlimited cellular data on any plan) |
| **Best for** | Suburban yards, budget-focused, reliable containment | Large acreage, "escape artists", urban canyons, dense canopy |

**Key takeaway for Halo:** Collar 5's differentiators are *continuous tracking* (AlwaysOn, 20 Hz) and *charging friction* (1 hr vs 2–3 hr) — not new training features. The training/correction system is identical across both. Precision+ (Skylark) is the accuracy lever, and it's subscription-gated, which is where the membership tiers matter.

## 2. Pack Membership Tiers — Bronze / Silver / Gold

All tiers include: unlimited cellular data, essential GPS, basic tracking, training content (Cesar Millan methodology). Billing: monthly, annual (11 mo paid), or 2-year (21 mo paid). 90-day money-back guarantee on membership.

| Feature | Bronze | Silver | Gold |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Monthly price** | $9.99 | $14.99 | $19.99 |
| **Fences (max)** | 5 | 20 | Unlimited |
| **GPS accuracy** | Improved (Precision+ tier) | Enhanced | Enhanced |
| **Activity tracking** | Basic | Enhanced — weekly + monthly reports | Enhanced — weekly + monthly reports |
| **Real-time tracking dashboard** | Basic | Yes | Yes |
| **Pack Update newsletter** | — | Yes | Yes |
| **Halo accessory discount** | — | 25% off | 50% off |
| **Live training sessions / expert Q&A** | — | — | Yes |
| **Early access to new products** | — | — | Yes |
| **Halo Care / Protection discount** | — | — | Save $50 on redemption |

**Note:** AlwaysOn GPS (20 Hz hardware) ships in Collar 5; *displaying* the highest-frequency live tracking and the real-time dashboard depends on the membership tier. Bronze caps at 5 fences and basic tracking — a natural upsell path to Silver/Gold for multi-dog or multi-property owners.
