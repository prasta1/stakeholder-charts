## Quick Take

Halo Collar is a high-growth pet tech company ($75M revenue in 2024, projected $100M+ in 2025) that makes GPS-enabled smart dog collars combining virtual fencing, training, and health tracking. Founded by IoT veterans Ken and Michael Ehrman with celebrity dog behaviorist Cesar Millan, they've scaled to 500K+ dogs and are expanding retail distribution in 2026. They're in a strong position as the market leader in GPS dog fences but face increasing competition from SpotOn and lower-cost alternatives.

### What They Do

Halo Collar makes a GPS-enabled smart dog collar that combines wireless virtual fencing, real-time GPS tracking, AI-powered training (with Cesar Millan's methodology), and health/activity monitoring. Subscription model ($9.99/mo+) for cellular data and cloud services. Sold direct and through Chewy, Amazon, Walmart, Best Buy.

## Competitive Landscape

| Competitor | Price | Key Differentiator |
|------------|-------|--------------------|
| **SpotOn Fence** | $999 | Most accurate GPS (dual-band antenna), no subscription, unlimited fences |
| **Tractive** | ~$50 | Budget GPS tracker, no fencing |
| **Invisible Fence** | Varies | Traditional wired in-ground system |
| **Pawtronic** | Varies | Newer entrant, wireless containment |

**Halo's Edge:** Training integration (Cesar Millan), combined fence + training + health in one device, largest installed base (500K+ dogs), strongest brand recognition in GPS fencing. Subscription revenue provides recurring income.

**Halo's Vulnerability:** Higher total cost of ownership vs. SpotOn (subscription fees), some accuracy complaints vs. SpotOn's dual-band system, customer service complaints visible in reviews.
