## Company background
JUMO is a family-owned industrial measurement and automation specialist (founded 1948) with 2,500+ employees, 25 subsidiaries, and a presence in 60+ countries, covering temperature/pressure/flow/liquid-analysis sensing, thyristor/SSR controllers, and process automation. The company is investing heavily in Single Pair Ethernet (SPE) — sensors that combine data, power, and direct cloud connectivity over a single two-wire cable — and is an active member of the SPE System Alliance, exhibiting in the SPE Pavilion at SPS Atlanta in September 2025 (source: jumo.group). Its hydroTRANS SPE sensor already streams temperature, humidity, and CO2 directly to the JUMO Cloud, signaling a strategic push toward connected, data-rich field devices.

## Expansion opportunities
JUMO's SPE-to-cloud strategy is a natural fit for on-device intelligence: anomaly detection and predictive maintenance running at the sensor/edge before data hits the cloud reduces bandwidth and latency — a clear EI value proposition.
