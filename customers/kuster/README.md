## Overview
Küster is an international automotive supplier specializing in intelligent electromechanical actuation and adjustment systems, in-house electronics, actuators, and drive cables. The company employs over 3,000 people across 16 companies on 4 continents, developing and manufacturing switching systems, actuators, and drives for major automotive manufacturers.
