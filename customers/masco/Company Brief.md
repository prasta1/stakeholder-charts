## Company background
Masco (founded 1929) is a global building-products leader with marquee brands including Behr paint, Delta faucets, KraftMaid cabinets, and Watkins Wellness (Hot Spring spas/hot tubs). The company is investing in digital/AI: Behr partnered with Google Cloud on the ChatHUE AI color-selection tool, and Masco reported Q1 2026 net sales up 6% to $1,918M (source: Masco SEC filings / StockTitan). Note recent leadership churn relevant to our champions: VJ Teenarsipur moved from President of Watkins Wellness to President of Behr (Oct 2025), with Steve Stigers now President of Watkins Wellness (Dec 2025), and a December 2025 executive-committee reorg placing top operating units directly under the CEO (source: BusinessWire/citybiz).

## Edge Impulse relationship
Mature, multi-use-case account in Optimization / Pre-production. Watkins Wellness (hot tub) use case is going well — models on 2 devices in lab testing, with the customer taking a model from development to deployment within the 30-day trial. ## Retention assessment
The renewal date (2026-05-27) has passed relative to today (2026-06-23) — this must be confirmed immediately as renewed, in-flight, or lapsed; an unconfirmed lapse on a healthy, expanding account is the most acute risk on this brief. Mitigating factors are strong: Neutral health (60), advanced stage, a successful deployed use case, and a proactive next-year pricing request all point to retention intent rather than churn. The clearest churn risk is the stalled Behr BYOM issue festering into frustration. The leadership reshuffle (Behr/Watkins presidents) also risks disrupting champion continuity.

## Expansion opportunities
This is the portfolio's strongest land-and-expand story. Angles: (1) close the second Behr use case once BYOM is unblocked; (2) move the Watkins hot-tub use case from lab to production/fleet deployment; (3) execute the multi-subsidiary expansion (Delta, KraftMaid, Hansgrohe) via Teague's exec outreach — Masco's scale makes an enterprise/MSA motion viable; (4) tie EI to Masco's stated AI/smart-product ambitions (e.g., Behr + Google Cloud) to elevate the relationship strategically.
