## Overview
Polar Electro is a Finnish manufacturer of sports training computers and wearable heart rate monitoring devices. The company pioneered the world's first wireless heart rate monitor and operates globally with approximately 1,200 employees and distribution in over 80 countries. Polar manufactures heart rate monitors, activity trackers, and training computers for athletic training and fitness applications.
