## Company background
Poly (originally Plantronics, founded 1961; merged with Polycom in 2018; acquired by HP for ~$1.7B in 2023) makes headsets, video bars, conference cameras and phones that integrate with Zoom/Teams/Google Meet. At InfoComm 2026 (June 2026) HP folded Poly into a unified AI-powered collaboration ecosystem, launching **on-device "Room Compute"** hardware that runs real-time AI — intelligent framing, active-speaker tracking, noise suppression — directly on dedicated neural hardware, plus DirectorAI multi-camera and VideoOS 5.1 (source below). This is squarely an on-device-ML direction that aligns with Edge Impulse's value proposition.

## Edge Impulse relationship
Poly is a **production, Advocacy-stage** account and an active **reference customer**: per the 10/14 note there was a recent greenlight on a marketing piece for a magazine, with renewal pricing in progress. The relationship is mature and commercialized — EI is embedded in shipping product and Poly is willing to be publicly referenceable.

## Retention assessment
The standout signal is the **tension between status and health**: Poly is at the most advanced lifecycle stage (Advocacy + Production) yet health is only Neutral/36 — meaning either the score is stale/under-maintained, or there is unsurfaced dissatisfaction beneath a strong surface relationship. With renewal not until 2027-01-29 there is runway, but a 36 on a reference account should be diagnosed, not ignored. The bigger strategic risk is HP's 2026 push toward **its own dedicated on-device neural hardware** (Room Compute / DirectorAI) — if Poly's edge-AI is increasingly handled by in-house HP silicon and software, EI must prove ongoing relevance in the model-development workflow or risk quiet displacement at the next cycle.

## Expansion opportunities
- **Leverage the advocacy momentum**: convert the magazine marketing piece into a formal case study / joint reference — strengthens the relationship and EI's own pipeline.
- **Map to the new Room Compute / DirectorAI roadmap**: position EI as the model-development and lifecycle toolchain feeding HP's on-device neural hardware (framing, speaker detection, noise/audio events) rather than competing with it.
- **Multi-product footprint**: the InfoComm portfolio (Studio 5/7, Focus 6 headsets) suggests additional audio/vision ML surfaces — explore expanding beyond the current single use case.
- **Account-tier uplift**: at $44k CARR with production embedment, there is headroom to grow seats/projects as Poly scales AI features across the line.

## Recommended next actions
