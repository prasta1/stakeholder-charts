# Poly (fka Plantronics, acquired by HP)

**Industry:** Unified Communications & Collaboration Hardware

**Website:** https://www.hp.com/us-en/poly.html

## Overview
HP Poly is a collaboration technology brand offering professional audio, video, and communication solutions for hybrid work environments, including headsets, video conferencing systems, office phones, cameras, and collaboration software. Formed through the 2018 merger of Plantronics and Polycom, then acquired by HP in 2023 for $1.7 billion, HP Poly solutions integrate seamlessly with Zoom, Microsoft Teams, and Google Meet.
