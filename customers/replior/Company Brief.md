## Company background

Publicly, CEO Christer Nilsson has positioned the company toward **digital endpoints, wearable/sensor data and gamification** to reduce patient dropout and improve compliance (source below) — which is the most plausible bridge between a clinical-trial SaaS firm and an edge-AI platform. Note: the broader 2025–2026 clinical-trials narrative is heavily AI/ML-themed, but on-device ML at the patient edge remains an emerging, not mainstream, use (sources below).
