# Replior AB

**Industry:** Clinical Trial Software / SaaS
**Headquarters:** Stockholm, Sweden
**Founded:** 2000
**Website:** https://www.replior.com/

## Overview
Replior develops Electronic Data Capture (EDC) and Electronic Patient Reported Outcomes (ePRO) software solutions for clinical trials. The company provides Trial Online, a secure, cloud-based platform compliant with FDA CFR 21 Part 11 that has been collecting data across 1000+ trials since 2000. Replior supports decentralized, hybrid, and site-based trial models for pharmaceutical companies and Contract Research Organizations (CROs).
