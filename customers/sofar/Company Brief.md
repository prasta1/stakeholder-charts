## Company background
Sofar Ocean (founded 2016 from the merger of Spoondrift + OpenROV) builds autonomous ocean-sensing hardware and maritime intelligence software — its flagship **Spotter** is a solar-powered ocean sensor for waves/wind/weather, complemented by the Wayfinder voyage-optimization platform. Recent public developments show a strong edge-AI trajectory: Sofar has added **on-device ML and edge acoustic processing to the Spotter platform** (e.g., the BOREALIS hydrophone and partner-built SCOUT-S PAM system for real-time underwater sound monitoring), and entered a CRADA with NOAA to feed Spotter data into wave-forecast models (sources below). This is one of the strongest natural edge-AI fits in the portfolio: remote, solar-powered, bandwidth-constrained sensors where on-device inference is essential.
