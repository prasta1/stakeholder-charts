# Sofar Ocean Technologies Inc

**Industry:** Ocean Technology / Marine Data Intelligence
**Headquarters:** San Francisco, California, USA
**Founded:** 2016 (formed by merger of Spoondrift and OpenROV)
**Website:** https://www.sofarocean.com/

## Overview
Sofar Ocean builds autonomous ocean sensing devices and maritime intelligence platforms that enable real-time environmental monitoring and voyage optimization. The company's flagship product, Spotter, is a compact solar-powered ocean sensor used by thousands of researchers, aquaculture operators, shipping companies, and climate scientists to monitor wave, wind, and weather conditions. Sofar's Wayfinder platform optimizes maritime shipping routes using ocean weather forecasting.
