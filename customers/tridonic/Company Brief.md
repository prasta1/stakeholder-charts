## Company background
Tridonic (founded 1960s) is the technology engine of the Zumtobel Group, supplying intelligent lighting, LED drivers, emergency lighting, controls, and sensor-based systems — roughly 80% of revenue from OEMs, with 1,687 employees in 70+ countries and FY22/23 sales of EUR 367M. The company is doubling down on smart, connected lighting: it ranked #2 in the European Patent Office for 2024 (71 applications), is expanding its Portugal R&D site explicitly toward intelligent lighting management, advanced sensors, predictive maintenance, and interoperability, and will showcase digital lighting at Light + Building 2026 in Frankfurt (source: z.lighting / portugalglobal.pt).
