# Tridonic GmbH & Co KG

**Industry:** Lighting Technology / LED Systems
**Headquarters:** Dornbirn, Austria
**Founded:** 1960s

## Overview
Tridonic is a world-leading supplier of intelligent lighting technology and a key subsidiary of the Zumtobel Group. The company develops and manufactures LED systems, LED drivers, and lighting management solutions across core competencies in LED/OLED, drivers, and connected lighting systems. With 1,687 employees, global presence in over 70 countries, and FY 2022/23 sales of EUR 367 million, Tridonic generates approximately 80% of revenues from the OEM market.
