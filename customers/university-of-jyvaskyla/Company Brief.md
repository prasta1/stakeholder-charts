## Company background
The University of Jyväskylä (founded 1863, jyu.fi) is Finland's largest multidisciplinary research university, with ~14,900 students and strengths in education, teacher training, and the human and natural sciences. Its Faculty of Information Technology is the first and one of the largest university-level IT research units in Finland, and the university runs an English-language two-year MSc in Artificial Intelligence (intakes through autumn 2025/2026). Faculty research touches edge intelligence, cyber-physical systems, and wearables/AI directly relevant to EI's platform — e.g. work on edge intelligence and privacy/security in cyber-physical systems, plus AI/wearable-robotics research themes ([JYU AI MSc](https://www.jyu.fi/en/study-with-us/artificial-intelligence-master-of-science-2-yrs-autumn-2025); [JYU Collective Intelligence group](https://www.jyu.fi/en/research-groups/collective-intelligence)).

## Retention assessment
Health is Neutral (35) — the weakest signal in this brief — but the renewal is greenlit and future-dated (2027-01-01), so there is no imminent contractual cliff. The risk is not a near-term renewal loss; it is stall-and-fade typical of low-touch academic accounts: a Neutral health score on a Stage 1 / Maturity 1 account that never advances past testing tends to lapse quietly when grant funding or a key faculty/student moves on. Concrete risks: (1) the renewal not actually getting signed because "RJ" never confirmed receipt — an administrative single point of failure; (2) no clear champion or anchored use case, so usage may be tied to one researcher or course; (3) low engagement keeping health stuck at Neutral. Direct read: the dollars are small and the renewal is procedural, but the account is one disengaged contact away from churn-by-neglect.

## Expansion opportunities
Expansion here is strategic, not primarily ARR-driven:
- **Research collaboration / co-marketing**: JYU's edge-intelligence and cyber-physical-systems research is an ideal joint-publication and case-study source — convert academic work into EI reference content.
- **Curriculum embedding**: Anchor EI into the MSc in AI and IT faculty coursework so each cohort trains on the platform — a durable, multi-year talent-pipeline and adoption channel.
- **Department land-and-expand**: Move beyond a single researcher to additional research groups (wearables/AI, collective intelligence) and labs.
- **Talent pipeline**: Position EI with graduating students who carry the tool into industry roles — long-term commercial seeding.
