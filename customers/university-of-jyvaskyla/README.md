## Overview
The University of Jyväskylä is Finland's largest multidisciplinary research university with ~14,900 students and expertise in education, human sciences, natural sciences, and teacher training. The university ranks in the top 2-3% of global research institutions and operates state-of-the-art campuses including structures designed by renowned Finnish architect Alvar Aalto.
