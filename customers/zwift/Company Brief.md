## Company background
Zwift (founded 2014) is an online fitness platform that gamifies indoor cycling/running, and increasingly a hardware maker — Zwift Ride smart frame, the Zwift Cog/Click virtual-shifting system, and bundles with Wahoo's KICKR CORE 2 (launched Sept 2025). Recent public moves are notably AI-forward: from November 2025 Zwift uses **AI to recommend routes, workouts and events with weekly auto-adjusting goals**, and it has partnered with TrainerRoad to combine AI-driven workout generation with Zwift's virtual world (sources below). The company is clearly investing in personalization/ML across software and connected hardware.

## Edge Impulse relationship
Zwift is a **high-engagement but demanding** customer at Optimization stage / Pre-production maturity. Per the CSM note, "Zwift continues to push the boundaries of the platform, near daily feature requests/bugs/etc." — strong product engagement and intensity, but at a cost: the assigned Solutions engineer (**Moe Sani is deactivated**), leaving a real coverage gap on a technically demanding account.

## Retention assessment
This is a **near-term, actionable risk**. Three factors compound: (1) renewal is only ~3 months out (**2026-09-30**); (2) health is Neutral/36 despite heavy engagement, which on a demanding account usually signals **support friction outpacing perceived value**; and (3) the **SE seat is vacant (deactivated user)** precisely when the customer is filing near-daily requests/bugs. A demanding customer without dedicated technical coverage heading into a renewal window is the highest-probability churn setup in this batch. The high request volume is a double-edged signal — it shows dependence on the platform, but unmet, it breeds frustration that lands hard at renewal.

## Expansion opportunities
- **AI personalization on connected hardware**: Zwift's Nov-2025 AI recommendations and TrainerRoad partnership suggest appetite for ML — position EI for on-device models in Zwift Ride / Cog / Click hardware (cadence, form, rider-state sensing).
- **Hardware line growth**: each new device (smart frame, virtual-shifting kits, trainer bundles) is a candidate surface for embedded ML — grow from a single program to a portfolio engagement.
- **Move from Pre-production to Production**: converting the current Optimization-stage work into a shipping deployment is the clearest CARR-expansion lever.
- **Reference potential**: a consumer-fitness brand success story is marketable for EI if a deployment ships.

## Recommended next actions
1. **Fix the coverage gap immediately**: reassign a Solutions engineer to replace deactivated Moe Sani — non-negotiable before the Sept renewal given request volume.
2. **Open the renewal motion now** (3 months out) and pair it with a service-recovery message acknowledging the support backlog.
3. **Triage the near-daily request/bug queue**: demonstrate responsiveness to reset the 36 health score ahead of renewal.
4. **Scope a production path**: identify the use case that moves Zwift from Pre-production to a shipping deployment and ties to CARR expansion.
5. **Align to Zwift's AI roadmap**: propose EI for on-device personalization across the Zwift Ride/Cog/Click hardware family.
